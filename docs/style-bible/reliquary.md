# Style bible: Reliquary

Status: **draft for gate A** (2026-11-07). Written from the faction board's Reliquary column and the approved Seer. The
machine-readable copy is `data/style-bible.json`; `tools/meshy/prompts.py` builds every prompt from it, so edit the JSON and
this page together.

Reference images (up to five go with every Reliquary prompt):

- `docs/concept-art/three-factions-board.png`, right column: the hero shot, the Seer, Wraith and Goliath tiles, close-ups.
- The approved Seer (project folder `renders/seer-composite/`): the canonical 3D look.

## The feel

Ancient relic machines. Watchful, gaunt and strange: pilgrims of a forgotten order, part machine and part relic, older than
the wars. Nothing is mass-produced; every piece looks carved, gilded and worn smooth by centuries.

## Motifs

Halo crowns of spikes. Hooded gothic masks with glowing teal eye slits. Long clawed fingers. Ribbed gilded limbs. Folded
bat-wing pauldron spars. Tattered banners, hanging lanterns and chains. Gothic arched reliquary cages around a soul-light.

## Silhouette

Tall, gaunt and long-limbed, stooped and reaching. The reverse of the Foundries: where they are low and wide, the Reliquary
is high and thin.

## Joints (for the print)

Gold ball knuckles in thick gilded sleeves (the Seer's standard). Thin Reliquary limbs are the hardest part to print, so every
class brief sets a minimum limb thickness and the cut map plans ball knuckles from the start.

## Never

Heavy boxy plate, stencilled numbers, hazard stripes, rust, red lacquer, smokestacks, industrial pipes, short stubby limbs.

## Class brief: Goliath (first walker under this bible)

Role: heavy (the faction's one towering walker). Frame: titan (size 4, 50 kit units, 75 mm printed at 1.5x). Stride 2, boiler 7,
slots 2 / 2 / 3 / 2.

- **Silhouette**: a pilgrim colossus. Long digitigrade legs with big ball-knuckle knees, a narrow reliquary-cage torso with a
  teal soul-light, a hooded mask under a large halo crown, folded bat-wing pauldron spars, a stooped reaching stance.
- **Weapons** (one item per arm): the Arc Cannon on the right forearm, a clawed left hand holding a Soul Lantern on a hooked staff.
- **Back**: two tall gilded spires, a tattered banner and hanging lantern chains.
- **Limbs**: gaunt but chunky. Every limb at least 7% of the body height thick.
